NLP · Transformers · LLM · Mar 2026

Review Intelligence Dashboard

Platform that turns Amazon product reviews into market reports: sentiment analysis, product categorisation and AI-written summaries.

  • Sentiment classification comparing three RoBERTa models: LoRA fine-tune, full fine-tune and a public baseline
  • Product categorisation into 10 classes with DistilRoBERTa fine-tuned on 70,000 Amazon reviews (82% accuracy)
  • Streamlit dashboard with streamed market reports via LangChain and OpenAI; models published on Hugging Face, packaged with Docker

NLP · Text classification · Mar 2026

Fake news classifier

Comparison of three NLP pipelines that label news articles as fake or real, trained on about 40,000 articles.

  • TF-IDF + Logistic Regression, Bag of Words + Naive Bayes and TF-IDF + Random Forest
  • Exploratory analysis of class balance, text length and n-grams
  • Test accuracy between 95% and 99.9%, with 5-fold cross-validation and an external validation set
